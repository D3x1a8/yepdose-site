Moving from Medisafe

Your medicines and your dose history can leave Medisafe in one file and arrive in Yepdose in a few minutes. This page covers the export, the import, what comes across and what does not, and what Yepdose charges for. Checked on 15 November 2026.

Yepdose is a medicine reminder for iPhone and Apple Watch. Unlimited medicines and reminders are free, there is no account, and importing is part of the free app. It is not on Android or the web. For a feature-by-feature table, see Yepdose vs Medisafe.

Before you start

You need an iPhone on iOS 17 or later with Yepdose from the App Store, and Medisafe still installed and signed in. Medisafe sends its report by email through Apple's Mail app, so Mail needs an account set up on the iPhone (Medisafe says so in its help article on export errors). Keep Medisafe's reminders on until every schedule in Yepdose is right.

1. Export your report from Medisafe

These are Medisafe's own steps for iPhone, from its help article How can I share a report of my medication history?

In Medisafe

Tap Manage in the bottom menu, then Report, then Send. Enter your own email address, choose the start and end dates, and send it. Medisafe says the report goes back up to one year and is a CSV file. You may also see this called Export to Excel; it is the same file.

On your iPhone

Open the email in Mail, touch and hold the attachment, and choose Save to Files. Remember where you saved it.

If Medisafe is locked

Outside the U.S., Medisafe has needed a subscription since 1 January 2026. Its help article on the change says people who can no longer open the app can still download a report of their history under Support and data management, with Export data. We have not tested that file. If Yepdose does not recognise it, add your medicines by pasting a list instead (step 2).

2. Import the file into Yepdose

Open the import screen

In Yepdose, go to Settings → Import medicines → Medisafe, and pick the file you saved. Reading this on the iPhone that has Yepdose 1.0.2 or later? Open the import screen straight from here, then tap Medisafe.

Review before anything is saved

Yepdose reads the file on your iPhone and shows a review list: one row for each medicine, with its name, its strength, the schedule Yepdose worked out, and how many past doses came with it. Tap a row to correct the name or strength. Untick any medicine you do not want. Nothing is saved until you tap Import.

Importing twice is safe

If you import the same file again, or a newer export later, doses are not duplicated. A medicine you already have with the same name and strength is reused, not added a second time. Doses you have already logged in Yepdose are kept as you logged them.

No file? Paste a list

Settings → Import medicines → Paste a list takes any text, one medicine per line, such as "Metformin 500 mg twice daily". This brings your medicines but not your history.

3. Check every medicine once

Medisafe's file is a log of doses, not a copy of your schedules. Yepdose works each schedule out from that log, so look at each medicine before you rely on it.

Days and times

Yepdose reads which days the doses fall on (every day, certain weekdays, every few days, or a cycle such as 21 days on and 7 off) and which times of day come up regularly. With fewer than seven days of history, or a pattern it cannot read, the review marks the schedule "guessed" and sets it to every day. Open the medicine and set the days and times you take it.

Amount and form

The file does not say how much you take, so every medicine arrives as one tablet per dose. If you take half a tablet, two capsules, drops or an injection, change it.

As-needed medicines

A medicine you take only when you need it arrives with fixed times. Change its schedule to As needed.

Dates

If your export writes dates day first (5/10/2026 for 5 October), look at a few past doses in History. Yepdose reads a file as day first when any date in it could only be day first; a file where every date could be read either way is read month first.

Then turn off the second set of reminders

Once every schedule is right, stop Medisafe's reminders so you are not asked twice: in the iPhone's Settings, go to Notifications → Medisafe and turn off Allow Notifications, or delete the app. If you pay for Medisafe through Apple, deleting the app does not end the subscription; that is under your name → Subscriptions in the iPhone's Settings.

What comes across, and what does not

In MedisafeIn Yepdose after the import
Medicine namesYes
StrengthYes, when it is written at the end of the name, as in "Metformin 500 mg". Otherwise add it in the review.
Doses recorded as taken, skipped or missedYes, with the time each was scheduled and the time it was recorded. They appear in History and count in the adherence figures.
Notes on a doseYes, kept with that dose
SchedulesWorked out from your history, not copied. Check each one.
Dose amount and formNo. Every medicine arrives as one tablet per dose.
Doses still pending or snoozedNo
A medicine with nothing recorded in the dates you exportedNo. Add it by hand, or export a longer period.
Measurements and trackersNo. Only medication rows are read.
Refill counts, appointments, doctors, MedfriendsNo. They are not in the file. In Yepdose, refill reminders and sharing with a caregiver are part of Family and are set up afresh.
History older than one yearNo. Medisafe's report goes back one year at most.

The file Yepdose reads has six columns: Type, Name, Recorded on, Scheduled for, Value and Notes. Anything Medisafe keeps outside those columns is not in it.

What is free in Yepdose, and what is paid

Free

Unlimited medicines and reminders. Follow-ups at 10, 30 and 60 minutes when you do not answer, and a missed mark in your history after two hours. One tap to log a dose from the notification, the Home Screen widget, the Apple Watch app or Siri. Every kind of schedule. Unlimited profiles on your phone, for a parent or a child. History, import, and iCloud sync between your own devices. No account, no ads, and nothing collected. The basics are free. That won't change.

Family, paid

$29.99 a year after a 14-day free trial, or $59.99 once (U.S. prices). Family is for what a second person benefits from: sharing a profile with a caregiver, a push on their phone when a dose is missed or late, a weekly summary, and PDF and CSV reports. It also has medicine lookup with FDA label information, refill reminders, scanning a printed label or prescription, and adding medicines by voice.

For comparison

Medisafe's help center says that outside the U.S. the app has required a subscription since 1 January 2026, at $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, and it lists unlimited medications among its Premium features. Those details may have changed since 15 November 2026.

What you give up

Medisafe runs on Android and the web and has a drug interaction checker. Yepdose is iPhone and Apple Watch only, and it has no interaction checker: Family shows FDA label text for information only. If someone in your household is on Android, Yepdose is the wrong choice for them today.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Yepdose has no sign-up. Your medicines stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Read the privacy policy

Where does my Medisafe file go?

Nowhere. It is read on your iPhone. Yepdose runs no server, and the App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Can a caregiver still be told when I miss a dose?

Yes, with Family. Settings → Share with a caregiver sends them an iCloud invitation; they need an iPhone too.

Is Yepdose a medical device?

No. It is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice, check interactions for you, or replace your doctor or pharmacist.
