Yepdose is a medicine reminder for iPhone and Apple Watch. It asks at the right time, asks again if you don't answer, and keeps a record you can trust. The basics are free. That won't change.

It asks. You tap.

It asks. You tap. It writes it down.

Add a medicine and the times you take it. At each time your phone asks. You answer with one tap: Took it, Remind me in 10 minutes, or Skip. Yepdose records the answer with the time and shows the day grouped by morning, afternoon and evening, so you can see at a glance what is done and what is next.

02

It follows up.

It follows up.

A reminder you swipe away is gone. Yepdose asks again at 10 minutes, at 30, and at 60. At 120 it stops asking and writes missed, so the gap shows in your history instead of disappearing. The window is yours to change.

Why 10, 30 and 60?

Ten minutes catches the dose you meant to take a moment ago. Thirty and sixty catch the one that slipped your mind. At two hours the app stops asking and writes it down instead. You can change every one of these times.

Snooze restarts the ladder.

Remind me in 10 min means exactly that. The follow-ups start over from there.

Quiet hours hold the follow-ups.

Set quiet hours and the follow-ups wait, unless you have flagged the medicine as critical. Reminders can be marked time-sensitive so they show through Focus.

Missed is a record, not a scolding.

Come back to a short sheet: what was missed, a reason if you want one, mark them all at once.

One tap, wherever you are.

You don't have to open the app. Answer from the notification, from a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, from the Apple Watch app or its complication, or ask Siri. While a dose is overdue, a Live Activity keeps a countdown on the Lock Screen until you answer.

06

No account, no server.

There is no account, because there is no server.

Your medicines live on your phone and in your own iCloud. We don't run a server. There are no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting kit. There is no third-party code in the app that talks to the internet. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected, and it says that because it is true.
