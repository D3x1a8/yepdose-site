Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026. Applies to the Yepdose app for iPhone and Apple Watch and to yepdose.com.

Short version: we don't collect your data. We can't. There is no account and no server of ours.

What the app stores

Your medicine list, schedules, dose history and profiles are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they are also stored in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. Apple's terms govern iCloud.

What we receive

Nothing. Yepdose contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK and no third-party code that talks to the internet. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Medicine lookup (Family)

If you use medicine lookup or the label check, the medicine name you type is sent over HTTPS to two US government services: the National Library of Medicine's RxNorm (rxnav.nlm.nih.gov) for names and strengths, and openFDA (api.fda.gov) for the manufacturer's label text. Nothing else is sent, nothing identifies you, and Yepdose stores nothing there. These features are optional; you can type a medicine name without them.

Family sharing

If you choose to share a profile with a caregiver, sharing happens through Apple's iCloud sharing between your accounts. We are not a party to it and cannot read what is shared. You can stop sharing at any time from the app.

Apple Health

If you choose to import from Apple Health (iOS 26 or later), Yepdose reads only the medicines you tick on Apple's permission sheet and their dose log, on your device, once, to build your list. It does not send Health data anywhere. Apple does not allow apps to write medication records to Health.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved for reminders. Caregiver alerts in the Family tier use Apple's CloudKit notifications between your own accounts.

Purchases

The Family tier is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

This website

yepdose.com is a static site with no cookies and no analytics. Its headline font is loaded from Google Fonts, which sees the request like any web host does. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose servers keep standard access logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you email us, we keep the message to answer it and delete it afterwards on request.

Children

Yepdose is not directed at children under 13. A parent may keep a child's profile within their own account; the data stays under the parent's control as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
