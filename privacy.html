Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026. Applies to the Yepdose app for iPhone and Apple Watch and to yepdose.com.

Short version: we don't collect your data. We can't. There is no account and no server of ours.

What the app stores

Your medicine list, schedules, dose history and profiles are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they are also stored in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. Apple's terms govern iCloud.

What we receive

Nothing. Yepdose contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDK and no third-party code that talks to the internet. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Medicine lookup (Family)

If you use medicine lookup or its label information, the medicine name you type is sent over HTTPS to two US government services: the National Library of Medicine's RxNorm (rxnav.nlm.nih.gov) for names and strengths, and openFDA (api.fda.gov) for the manufacturer's label text. Nothing else is sent, nothing identifies you, and Yepdose stores nothing there. These features are optional; you can type a medicine name without them.

Family sharing

If you choose to share a profile with a caregiver, sharing happens through Apple's iCloud sharing between your accounts. We are not a party to it and cannot read what is shared. You can stop sharing at any time from the app.

Apple Health

If you choose to import from Apple Health (iOS 26 or later), Yepdose reads only the medicines you tick on Apple's permission sheet and their dose log, on your device, once, to build your list. It does not send Health data anywhere. Apple does not allow apps to write medication records to Health.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete everything removes your medicines, schedules, history and profiles from the device and, when iCloud is on, from your private iCloud database; the deletion then reaches your other devices. It also ends any sharing you started. A profile that someone else shares with you belongs to them: it is not deleted, and you can leave it at any time from Settings → Share with a caregiver. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. You can also remove Yepdose's iCloud data from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved for reminders. Caregiver alerts in the Family tier use Apple's CloudKit notifications between your own accounts.

Purchases

The Family tier is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

This website

yepdose.com is a static site with no cookies and no tracking scripts. Its fonts are served from yepdose.com itself, so pages make no requests to anyone else. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web server, both keep standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) under GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy. We look only at Cloudflare's totals: how many visits, which pages and which countries. They do not identify anyone. We also use Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, which report how the site appears in their search results; they add nothing to these pages. None of this touches the app, which collects nothing.

Email

If you email us, we keep the message to answer it and delete it afterwards on request.

Children

Yepdose is not directed at children under 13. A parent may keep a child's profile within their own account; the data stays under the parent's control as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
